## Simple Structure Components

- **Bolt** - A single, narrow projectile. Hits one target.
- **Burst** - A short-range explosion, hits all targets within 5 ft.
- **Touch** - Must be delivered by touch. Bypasses ranged defenses.
- **Flash** - Instantaneous flash of light or energy. Brief and disruptive.
- **Spray** - Cone-shaped effect up to 10 ft.
- **Beam** - A line 20 ft long, 5 ft wide.
- **Ring** - A circular effect that radiates out from the caster.
- **Gust** - Directed cone of force, primarily for push or environmental control.
- **Drop** - Falls from above onto target area. Ignores cover.
- **Snap** - Instant, minor spatial distortion at touch range.

## Basic Structure Components

- **Wave** - A traveling wall of energy (30 ft long, 5 ft thick).
- **Chain** - Jumps to 1 additional target per 2 dice spent.
- **Field** - 10 ft radius sphere or dome with ongoing effect.
- **Lance** - Pierces in a straight line, ignores half cover.
- **Column** - Vertical burst or beam 10 ft in diameter.
- **Spread** - Wide angle spray, 15 ft cone.
- **Pulse** - Radiates outward 10 ft per 2 dice spent.
- **Trap** - Triggered effect in a set area, up to 10 ft.
- **Line** - 5 ft wide, 40 ft long directed attack.
- **Veil** - Obscuring or distorting effect in a 10 ft cube.

## Intermediate Structure Components

- **Barrage** - Multiple targeted bolts (up to 3 targets per 3 dice).
- **Dome** - 20 ft radius shield or energy field.
- **Cascade** - Repeated bursts over multiple rounds.
- **Maw** - Devouring area with restraining or damaging effect.
- **Web** - Spread over a wide area and restricts movement.
- **Spiral** - Rotating or expanding field over time.
- **Surge** - Sudden spike in area up to 30 ft.
- **Blade Wall** - Wall 30 ft long that deals damage on contact.
- **Swarm** - Many small entities attacking simultaneously.
- **Collapse** - Sudden spatial or elemental collapse in 10 ft radius.

## Advanced Structure Components

- **Rift** - Tears a temporary hole in space, 15 ft radius.
- **Torrent** - Sustained area of violent energy (30 ft diameter).
- **Inferno** - Expanding effect that grows 10 ft per round.
- **Zone** - Persistent zone with escalating effects.
- **Crucible** - Seals creatures within for X rounds.
- **Echo** - Repeats the spell once, one round later.
- **Beacon** - Anchored effect that pulses every round.
- **Disjunction** - Cancels all magical effects in 20 ft radius.
- **Howl** - Area-wide mind-affecting effect (fear, rage, etc.).
- **Storm** - Chaotic, persistent area with unpredictable effects.

## Master Structure Components

- **Cataclysm** - Devastating area effect (up to 100 ft radius).
- **Ascension** - Alters or removes fundamental physical laws.
- **Eternity** - Ongoing effect for hours or days.
- **Convergence** - Combines multiple effects into one.
- **Terraform** - Alters large-scale terrain (1 sq mile or more).
- **Wyrmhole** - Portal linking distant locations.
- **Genesis** - Creates a persistent entity or construct.
- **Eclipse** - Blanket effect over vast area (e.g. darken sky).
- **Sundering** - Breaks or shatters magical bonds or barriers.
- **Vortex** - Persistent whirl or pull that draws in all nearby matter.

## Optional Structure Components (All Tiers)

- **Delay** - Spell activates X rounds after casting.
- **Sustain** - Spell continues each round for AP spent.
- **Redirect** - Target can be changed mid-cast.
- **Suspend** - Hold the spell until triggered.
- **Anchor** - Locks effect to an object or place.
- **Channel** - Allows continuous control after cast.
- **Echo** - Duplicates the effect once after delay.
- **Blend** - Mixes two different effects together.
- **Focus** - Narrows the area or target for bonus dice.
- **Scatter** - Randomizes targets within an area.
